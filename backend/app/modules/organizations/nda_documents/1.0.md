# Auracles Platform Confidentiality Agreement

**Version 1.0**

This Agreement applies to you as a member of an organization that performs
Attestation work on Auracles. You are asked to accept it once, and again
whenever a new version is published.

## 1. What this covers

"Confidential Information" means anything you are given access to in order to
carry out Attestation work that is not already public, including:

- Frameworks, Artifacts, and any drafts, source files, or supporting material
  attached to them;
- the identity of a Contributor or Operator where it is not published;
- Attestation briefs, reviewer notes, rubric scores, and reports before they
  are published;
- commercial terms, pricing, and transaction details you encounter.

It does not cover information that is already public through no act of yours,
that you held before receiving it, or that you develop independently without
reference to it.

## 2. What you agree to

- Use Confidential Information only to perform the Attestation work assigned
  to you, and for no other purpose.
- Do not disclose it to anyone outside your organization, and inside your
  organization only to people staffed on the same work.
- Do not copy, retain, or move it outside the platform except where the
  platform provides for it.
- Do not use it to compete with, replicate, or derive a product from the
  Framework under review.
- Protect it with at least the care you use for your own confidential
  material.

## 3. Independence

You must declare any interest that could affect your judgement on a review,
including a relationship with the Contributor or Operator, or a stake in a
competing Framework. If you are unsure whether something counts, declare it.

## 4. Duration

These obligations start when you accept this Agreement and continue for three
years after your last access to the Confidential Information concerned. Where
information is a trade secret, the obligations continue for as long as it
remains one.

## 5. Return and deletion

When your work ends, or on request, delete or return everything you hold
outside the platform. Nothing here requires you to delete records you must
keep by law.

## 6. No licence

Access is not a licence. No right in any Framework, Artifact, or trade mark
passes to you or your organization under this Agreement.

## 7. Breach

Unauthorised disclosure may cause harm that money alone cannot repair, so
Auracles and the affected Contributor or Operator may seek injunctive relief
in addition to any other remedy. Auracles may suspend your access to
Attestation work while a suspected breach is investigated.

## 8. Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria, and
the courts of Nigeria have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising
from it.
