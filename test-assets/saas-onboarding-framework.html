SaaS Customer Onboarding Framework

Version 1.0 — A repeatable system for activating new B2B SaaS accounts in their first 30 days.

1. Purpose

This framework gives a Customer Success team a structured, measurable path from contract-signed to first-value-realized. It replaces ad-hoc onboarding emails with a staged playbook tied to activation metrics.

2. The Four Activation Stages

Stage 1 — Welcome & Account Setup (Day 0–2)

  • Send personalized welcome within 1 business hour of signature.
  • Provision workspace, invite admin, confirm SSO/domain.
  • Schedule the kickoff call before Day 3.

Stage 2 — Kickoff & Goal Mapping (Day 3–7)

  • Run a 45-minute kickoff: confirm the customer's primary success metric.
  • Map their goal to a single "first win" milestone.
  • Assign a named CSM and share the 30-day plan.

Stage 3 — Guided Adoption (Day 8–21)

  • Drive the customer to complete the 3 core actions that predict retention.
  • Weekly check-in: blocker review + usage snapshot.
  • Trigger in-app nudges for any unfinished core action.

Stage 4 — First Value & Expansion Signal (Day 22–30)

  • Confirm the "first win" metric was hit; document the outcome.
  • Run a value-review call; capture a quote or NPS.
  • Flag expansion or risk signals for the account owner.

  • CSM — owns the 30-day plan and all customer touchpoints.
  • Onboarding Specialist — handles technical setup and integrations.
  • Account Owner — receives expansion/risk signals at Day 30.
